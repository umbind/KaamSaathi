# KaamSaathi Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: September 2026  
**Governing Statute**: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 & IT Act, 2000  
**Jurisdiction**: Republic of India  

At **KaamSaathi (कामसाथी)**, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act")** and applicable Indian information technology regulations.

---

## 1. What Personal Data We Collect

1.1 **Identity & Contact Data**: Mobile phone number (verified via SMS OTP), full name, preferred language (Hindi or English).  
1.2 **Location Data**: District in Uttar Pradesh, locality name, and postal PIN code. Exact GPS coordinates are collected only with your explicit device permission when submitting a request or setting provider coverage.  
1.3 **Provider KYC Documents**: Copies of government photo ID (Voter ID, Driving Licence, or Masked e-Aadhaar where only the last 4 digits are visible), and trade or police clearance certificates voluntarily uploaded for badge review.  
1.4 **Transactional Metadata**: Quote amounts, change orders, booking timestamps, ratings, and customer reviews.

---

## 2. Gated Contact Privacy Protocol (APP-002)

2.1 **Contact Masking**: Your mobile phone number is **never displayed publicly** in search results, open listings, or provider directories.  
2.2 **Mutual Reveal Gating**: A customer's phone number is shared with a Provider **only after** the customer explicitly accepts that Provider's quote in the app.  
2.3 **Anti-Scraping**: Automated scraping of user profiles or telephone numbers is blocked via IP-based rate limiting and token bucket throttling.

---

## 3. How We Use and Process Your Data

We process your personal data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:
- To authenticate your account via secure one-time passwords (OTP).
- To match customer repair requests with nearby verified technicians.
- To maintain an immutable audit trail of bookings, disputes, and safety escalations.
- To comply with statutory summons or requests from law enforcement under Indian law (e.g. UP Police under CrPC Section 91).

---

## 4. Storage Security and Encryption (AES-256-GCM)

4.1 **Data in Transit**: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using **TLS 1.3** (Strict HTTPS with HSTS).  
4.2 **Data at Rest**: Sensitive document uploads and identity records are encrypted at rest using **AES-256-GCM** with unique initialization vectors.  
4.3 **Session Security**: Mobile and web sessions utilize **Refresh Token Rotation (RTR)**. Any attempt to replay or reuse an expired token immediately revokes all active sessions for that account.

---

## 5. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

You possess the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:
- **Right to Access**: You may view your profile, request history, and reviews at any time within the app.
- **Right to Correction**: You may update inaccurate personal or location details.
- **Right to Erasure**: You may request the deletion of your account and personal data by writing to our Grievance Officer, subject to mandatory statutory record retention requirements (e.g., dispute logs, completed transaction records).
