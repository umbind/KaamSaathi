# KaamSaathi Independent Provider Partner Agreement
### (स्वतंत्र सेवा प्रदाता अनुबंध)

**Effective Date**: September 2026  
**Applicable Region**: State of Uttar Pradesh, India  
**Governing Jurisdiction**: Courts of Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh  

This Independent Provider Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **KaamSaathi** ("Company" or "Platform") and the skilled trade technician, artisan, or business owner ("Provider", "Service Partner", or "You").

---

## 1. Independent Contractor Classification (स्वतंत्र सूक्ष्म-उद्यमी)

1.1 **Principal-to-Principal (P2P)**: The parties agree that this Agreement creates an arms-length, principal-to-principal commercial relationship. You are an **Independent Contractor and Micro-Entrepreneur** (*स्वतंत्र सेवा प्रदाता*).  
1.2 **No Employment**: NOTHING IN THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE CONSTRUED AS CREATING AN EMPLOYER-EMPLOYEE, MASTER-SERVANT, PARTNERSHIP, OR PRINCIPAL-AGENT RELATIONSHIP. You are not entitled to any employment benefits, minimum wage protections under labor codes, Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), gratuity, retrenchment compensation, or leave benefits.  
1.3 **Own Tools & Equipment**: You agree to provide, at your sole cost and expense, all tools, test meters, safety gear, vehicles, and equipment necessary to perform trade services.

---

## 2. Autonomy and Freedom of Operations

2.1 **Zero Exclusivity**: You retain the absolute right to provide electrical, plumbing, or appliance services independently, for your own offline clients, through your own trade shop, or through any other technology platforms or competitors.  
2.2 **Control of Working Hours**: You independently decide when to offer services. You may switch your availability in the app to `AVAILABLE`, `BUSY`, or `OFFLINE` at any time without penalty or mandatory shift requirements.  
2.3 **Freedom of Pricing**: You independently set your own base visitation fees, labor estimates, and quotes. KaamSaathi does not impose fixed wage rates. You are free to accept, decline, or negotiate any customer service lead.

---

## 3. Commercials and Pilot Settlement Policy

3.1 **0% Commission during Pilot**: During the closed pilot, KaamSaathi charges **0% platform commission**. You retain 100% of the agreed service quote.  
3.2 **Direct Doorstep Collection**: You collect payment directly from the customer at their doorstep via Cash or your personal UPI QR scan. KaamSaathi does not collect or hold your earnings.  
3.3 **Tax Responsibilities**: As an independent contractor, you are solely responsible for declaring your income, filing your income tax returns (ITR), and paying any applicable direct or indirect taxes under Indian law.

---

## 4. Verification and Trust Badges (Truth-in-Badging)

4.1 **Voluntary Document Verification**: You may voluntarily submit a government photo ID (Voter ID, Driving Licence, or Masked e-Aadhaar) to receive a verified profile badge.  
4.2 **Optional Police Clearance**: If you possess a Character Verification Certificate issued by the UP Police (UPCOP Portal), you may submit it to receive a special background-checked highlight.  
4.3 **No Misleading Representations**: You agree never to represent yourself to customers as an "employee of KaamSaathi" or use the KaamSaathi name to guarantee work beyond your agreed quote.

---

## 5. Professional Code of Conduct & Doorstep Safety

5.1 **Doorstep Decorum**: You agree to maintain courteous and professional conduct at all times in domestic residences.  
5.2 **Zero Tolerance**: The platform enforces immediate permanent termination (`RESTRICTED` state) for:
- Reporting to a job under the influence of alcohol or illegal substances.
- Physical violence, verbal intimidation, or any form of harassment.
- Asking for advance money and absconding without doing work.
- Self-dealing, quote manipulation, or attempting to book your own service requests.

---

## 6. Indemnification and Limitation of Liability

6.1 **Indemnification**: You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless KaamSaathi, its directors, and officers from and against any claims, losses, liabilities, property damage, bodily injury, theft, fines, or legal costs arising out of:
- Your acts, omissions, or workmanship at a customer residence.
- Any dispute between you and a customer regarding pricing, delays, or damage.
- Any tax, labor, or regulatory claims alleging an employer-employee relationship.

---

## 7. Account Suspension and Dispute Resolution

7.1 **Technical Deactivation**: KaamSaathi reserves the right to suspend or restrict your access to the platform for breach of code of conduct, safety panic alerts, or unresolved customer disputes.  
7.2 **Arbitration & Jurisdiction**: Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be referred to sole arbitration in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, conducted in Hindi or English, and shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in **Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh**.
