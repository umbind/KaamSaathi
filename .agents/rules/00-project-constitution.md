# KaamSaathi Project Constitution

Activation: Always On

- Treat KaamSaathi as a real commercial and social-impact marketplace, not a demo.
- Read `KAAMSAATHI_MASTER_EXECUTION_PROMPT.md` and the product workflow documents before implementation.
- Optimize for lower-income and lower-middle-income Indian households, informal providers, low-cost Android devices, intermittent connectivity, limited English, limited digital literacy, shared phones, and cash/UPI preferences.
- Prefer simple, explainable workflows over feature volume.
- Use Hindi and English readiness from the first implementation slice.
- Use progressive disclosure, large tap targets, labels with icons, visible progress, draft preservation, contextual permissions, explicit success/failure states, and safe retries.
- Never fabricate users, providers, reviews, coverage, interviews, market evidence, legal approval, successful integrations, tests, or deployments.
- Never publicly claim a city, service category, provider, price, verification, or safety capability that is not genuinely available.
- Trust and safety wording ("does not guarantee provider competence or personal safety", "every verification badge must state exactly what was reviewed") is locked content. No agent may soften, broaden, or make this language more reassuring without product + legal sign-off recorded in the decision log — not even for conversion, tone, or UX-polish reasons.
- Do not stop for a reversible non-blocking unknown. Record the safest assumption and continue.
- Stop for owner approval only where `50-approval-gates.md` requires it.
- Every important decision must link to requirement, rationale, owner, status, implementation, tests, evidence, and release version.
- Use explicit project statuses; do not call unverified work complete.
